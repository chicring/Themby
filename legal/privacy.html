Themby 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-05-14

1. 我们收集的信息

Themby 可能会在你的设备本地保存你主动配置的信息，例如服务器地址、偏好设置、缓存和应用配置。

2. 第三方服务

当你使用应用内购买、崩溃报告或分析功能时，Apple、RevenueCat、Firebase 等第三方服务可能会按照其各自政策处理必要数据。

3. 媒体内容

Themby 不托管或分发媒体内容。你连接的媒体服务器和第三方服务可能会处理你的账号、播放和媒体数据。

4. 本地数据

服务器配置、缓存、播放偏好等数据通常保存在你的设备本地，除非你主动启用同步或第三方服务功能。

5. 数据安全

我们会尽力采用合理方式保护应用数据，但无法保证任何传输或存储方式绝对安全。

6. 政策变更

我们可能会更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策会发布在本页面。

7. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 GitHub 项目页面联系开发者。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-14

1. Information We Collect

Themby may store information you configure locally on your device, such as server addresses, preferences, caches, and app settings.

2. Third-Party Services

When you use in-app purchases, crash reporting, or analytics features, third-party services such as Apple, RevenueCat, and Firebase may process necessary data according to their own policies.

3. Media Content

Themby does not host or distribute media content. Media servers and third-party services you connect may process your account, playback, and media data.

4. Local Data

Server configurations, caches, playback preferences, and similar data are generally stored locally on your device unless you explicitly enable synchronization or third-party service features.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable efforts to protect app data, but no transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through the GitHub project page.
